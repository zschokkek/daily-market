HOW TO PLAY

  1. Guess the secret Kalshi market of the day.
  2. Pool: sports futures always + single games only if today/live + all politics/business/prices/weather/culture open. House districts are bunched per state — NYHOUSE = New York House Races (custom 2-way D vs R, 2 strikes, shows one district when correct).
  3. Six broads: sports · politics (SENATE / HOUSE / GOV / PRES / TRUMP / ELECT) · business (TECH/FINANCE/CORP) · prices (crypto/CMDTY only on $) · weather (HEAT/RAIN/HURRICANE) · culture (MUSIC/FILM/TV & CELEB). Abbreviations show as tags: NRSC → Republican.
  4. After each guess: green = exact, yellow = close (e.g., market name shares ≥2 words, same region for LOC). Arrows: ▲/▼ = higher/lower (price/volume/strikes), ←/→ = earlier/later (dates). LOC=state/country (N/A for prices). EXPIRES as Aug 08, 26. Tip: LOC yellow with no arrow → likely US national (NY vs US); two arrows → different country (NY → Israel = ↓→); * → World/global (World contains country).
  5. STRIKES = #outcomes (custom House bunches like NYHOUSE = 2, grouped margin/turnout keep mode: House Margin 9, House Turnout 5). PRICE = favorite’s odds (¢) — highest yes price; bunched markets (CAHOUSE / CAMOV / CAVOTETURN) = volume-weighted avg, live-refreshed on open. VOLUME = total $ volume — bunched = sum across districts in state.
  6. 10 tries. Share copies with a sleek “Copied” toast for 2s.
